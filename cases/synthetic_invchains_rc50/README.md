## Notes

Chains of 10 inverters whose nets are RC lines of 100 segments with coupling capacitors to the same net of the next chain, as a layout extraction writes them: 50 RC nodes per transistor, the chains tied into one matrix by the coupling. The totals per net (200 Ohm, 2 fF to ground, 2 fF to the next chain) do not depend on the number of segments. Transistors: sg13_lv_nmos and sg13_lv_pmos of IHP SG13G2 (PSP103 through OSDI), typical corner. Generated (circref/generate.py); size: chains, 20 transistors each.
