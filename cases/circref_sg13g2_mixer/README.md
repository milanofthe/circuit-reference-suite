## Notes

A transconductor at the RF port under a differential pair switched by the LO, loads of 1 kOhm and 1 pF. RF 2.4 GHz with 50 mV amplitude, LO 2.5 GHz with 300 mV amplitude per side: the intermediate frequency of 100 MHz is v(ifp) - v(ifn).
375 LO cycles, 15 periods of the intermediate frequency.
Written for this suite on the IHP SG13G2 open PDK (PSP103 through OSDI, typical corner), in ngspice's and in VACASK's syntax.
