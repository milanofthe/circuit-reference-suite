## Notes

An NMOS pair on two tanks of 2 nH (2 Ohm in series) and 400 fF with a tail current of 0.5 mA, near 5.4 GHz. A current pulse between the outputs starts the oscillation.
200 periods.
Written for this suite on the IHP SG13G2 open PDK (PSP103 through OSDI, typical corner), in ngspice's and in VACASK's syntax.
