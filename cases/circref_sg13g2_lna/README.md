## Notes

Common-source stage with 0.5 nH in the source and 12 nH in the gate, cascode, a load tank of 5 nH and 0.8 pF with 500 Ohm in parallel: tuned to 2.4 GHz. Driven from 50 Ohm with 10 mV at 2.4 GHz.
Analyses: operating point, AC response from 100 MHz to 10 GHz, 360 periods of the carrier.
Written for this suite on the IHP SG13G2 open PDK (PSP103 through OSDI, typical corner), in ngspice's and in VACASK's syntax.
