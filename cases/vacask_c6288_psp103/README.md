## Notes

From the VACASK benchmark suite (`benchmark/README.md` there describes the setup).
The VACASK netlist uses ngspice's models distilled to Verilog-A; ngspice, Xyce and
Gnucap use their built-in models.

Xyce; ngspice and VACASK load it as OSDI. Upstream also has option variants of the
VACASK and ngspice decks (`vacask-ieb`, `vacask-nocb`, `vacask-nortc`, `ngspice-sparse`),
