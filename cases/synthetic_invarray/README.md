## Notes

An array of rows of 4 inverters, every net an RC line of 2 segments with coupling capacitors to the same net of the next row: one small cell repeated, 1 RC node per transistor. Not a memory: it stands for the size and repetition of one, without the bistable cell. Transistors: sg13_lv_nmos and sg13_lv_pmos of IHP SG13G2 (PSP103 through OSDI), typical corner.
