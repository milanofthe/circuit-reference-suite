## Notes

Clocked at 1 GHz. The differential input is a triangle of +-100 mV with a period of 40 clock cycles: q follows the clock while v(inp) > v(inn), qb while it is below.
80 clock cycles of 1 ns.
Written for this suite on the IHP SG13G2 open PDK (PSP103 through OSDI, typical corner), in ngspice's and in VACASK's syntax.
