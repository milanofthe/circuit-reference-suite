## Notes

NMOS input pair, PMOS mirror load, PMOS common-source second stage, Miller compensation with a nulling resistor. The output is fed back to the inverting input. Every transistor is drawn in fingers of 2 um.
Sized for 20 uA in each input transistor and 80 uA in the second stage at 1.2 V. Analyses: operating point, DC transfer of the buffer, closed-loop AC response, step response.
Written for this suite on the IHP SG13G2 open PDK (PSP103 through OSDI, typical corner), in ngspice's and in VACASK's syntax.
