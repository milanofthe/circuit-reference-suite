## Notes

Diode-connected 3.3 V NMOS, 1 pF pump capacitors on two complementary 20 MHz clocks, 10 pF and 2 MOhm at the output: clock edges of 1 ns against a settling time of microseconds.
200 clock cycles of 50 ns.
Written for this suite on the IHP SG13G2 open PDK (PSP103 through OSDI, typical corner), in ngspice's and in VACASK's syntax.
